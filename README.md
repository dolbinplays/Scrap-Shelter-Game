# Scrap Shelter v0.26.06.03.1828 - Forced Queue + Ghost Preview

Stage 1 prototype patch focused on puzzle readability and feel.

## Changes
- The puzzle now uses a forced queue: the left piece is always the active piece.
- The other two pieces are previews of upcoming parts.
- Players no longer choose among three pieces.
- Hovering/moving over the puzzle board shows a ghost footprint of the active part.
- Valid ghost placement uses the selected part/resource color.
- Invalid placement shows a red blocked footprint.
- Instructions and status text now explain the forced queue and preview behavior.

## How to play
1. Open the Puzzle tab.
2. Look at the left part labeled Place now.
3. Move over the board to preview the part footprint.
4. Tap/click where the footprint fits.
5. The queue advances and the next preview becomes the active piece.
6. Fill rows/columns to clear tiles into salvage.
7. Claim salvage to return resources to the base.
